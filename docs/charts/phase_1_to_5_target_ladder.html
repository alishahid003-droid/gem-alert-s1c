Phase 1–5 target ladder

Trigger, cashout and reseed for each phase, with Monte Carlo days-to-clear at three win rates (1,500 simulated runs per scenario, bootstrapped from real historical compound+sprint trade outcomes). Phase 1 runs on Compound alone; Sprint joins from Phase 2 on.

On the $3,000-in-24-hours hope: at a realistic Curve C-sized trade pace, Phase 1 clears in a median of 1.4–3.1 days across all three win rates, not 24 hours — getting there inside a day would need an early run of wins well above the model's middle case, which is possible but not the number to plan around. Every other figure here holds whether Phase 1 takes a day or four.
On the grand total: it's three separate pools of money added together, not one figure. The ladder-leg bankings (~$338k median) are profit pulled out at each of the 6 rungs inside every phase, as combined equity climbs — a different mechanism from, and in addition to, the 4 phase cashouts (~$126k, fixed amounts at each phase's trigger). The rest (~$1.04M median) is still live, uncashed equity the moment Phase 5 crosses $1,000,000. Add the three and the total lands near $1.5M, not the ~$1.16M an earlier version of this chart showed before it was corrected to include the ladder legs.
Bust rate: 0% across all three scenarios, every phase. Trade pace: 19/day compound, 9/day sprint (confirmed real pace, Robinhood + Base). Ladder-leg bankings and phase cashouts are two distinct, additive money flows — see breakdown above.
